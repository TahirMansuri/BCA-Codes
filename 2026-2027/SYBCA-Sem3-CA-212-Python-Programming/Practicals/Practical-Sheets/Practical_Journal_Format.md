# 📝 Practical Journal Sheet Format (Standard Guidelines)
### Department of Computer Applications (BCA) | IMRD, Shahada

Students are instructed to follow this standard format while writing their practical assignments in their practical journals:

---

## 📌 Standard Practical Sheet Structure:

1. **Practical Number & Title**: Specific title corresponding to the syllabus list.
2. **Date of Performance & Date of Submission**: Filled during lab sessions.
3. **Aim / Problem Statement**: Clear statement of what the program accomplishes.
4. **Objectives**: Key programming concepts demonstrated (e.g. data types, loops, recursion).
5. **Algorithm / Step-by-Step Logic**: Pseudocode or numbered logic steps.
6. **Program Source Code**: Cleanly indented code with comments.
7. **Sample Input & Output**: Exact console outputs for minimum 2 test cases.
8. **Viva Questions & Answers**: 3 to 5 core conceptual questions related to the practical.
9. **Faculty Signature & Remarks**: Space for grade and sign.
